Welcome to Avon Oaks

A private club, minutes from home

For decades, Avon Oaks has been where members of Northeast Ohio gather to play, dine, and celebrate — the course, the courts, the pool, and the table, all under one membership and kept to the standard our members expect.

The Golf

A course worth the membership alone

A championship 7,002-yard layout defined by mature oaks, narrow fairways, newly renovated bunkers, and lightning-fast greens — designed by Harold Paddock and refined by Geoffrey Cornish and Brian Huntley. A professional staff keeps members playing their best, from a first lesson to the club championship.

The Table

Dining, celebrations, and the club calendar

Member dining that feels like an occasion, and a setting made for weddings, holidays, and gatherings of every size. It's the part of the club members plan their year around.
